# Example questions

These prompts illustrate the planned MYNVENTORY integration. They do not work as an installation method. All names below are fictional.

- “Find the items I shared with ‘desk’ in the name.”
- “Which shared items have a recorded warranty ending in the next 30 days?”
- “Suggest changing Demo Desk Lamp to the Lighting category.”
- “Is that category suggestion still waiting for my review?”

A missing item may not be selected or synchronized. A pending suggestion does not mean a change has been saved. Review and approve it in the app.
